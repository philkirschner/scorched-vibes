How To Play

Controls

Pick a weapon, adjust angle and power, optionally spend fuel to move, then fire. Arrow keys adjust aim and power. Space fires.

Battle Rules

Wind pushes shots sideways. Gravity changes the arc. Shield absorbs damage before health. Fuel is spent one point per move and does not refill during the battle.

Battle Setup Options

  • Gravity — Normal is standard. Low makes shots float longer and arc wider. High makes them drop fast and hit hard.
  • Terrain — Sets the landscape shape and visual theme. Snowy Mountains and Volcano are jagged. Moon is bowl-shaped. Jungle has a central ridge. Arctic and Candy Land use stepped plateaus. Plains and Desert roll gently.
  • Vertical Spread — Balanced places both tanks at similar heights. Uneven or Extreme can put one tank much higher than the other.
  • Distance — Small is the classic map. Medium and Large zoom out so the tanks start farther apart. Shot power is boosted slightly on Medium and Large to match.
  • Wind — On means a random wind blows every battle and pushes shots sideways. Off turns wind off completely.
  • Aiming Help — On shows dotted trajectory hints so you can see roughly where your shot will go. Off hides them for a harder challenge.
  • Testing Mode — Gives both players unlimited coins and unlocks every weapon at the start of the battle. Great for trying things out.
